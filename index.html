Selected Work
01
Web Graphics UI Design

Churp

Problem: Informal, inconsistent processing of paperwork by community managers, and lack of formal support structure for resident gardeners. We aimed to digitise the management of community gardens to connect Tengah residents and community managers.

Process: Industry research was done on existing government websites to mimic their familiar style. This allowed me to surmise my approach into two keywords: "inclusive" and "welcoming". The graphics were first drafted in Adobe Photoshop, then translated into SVG web assets in Figma. Finally, they were optimised and minified to maintain a small size. Throughout the process, feedback was gathered from the developer side to iterate upon with. The final designs ensured all the developers were satisfied.

Outcome: 13 fully polished, minified SVGs ranging from large web assets, to icons, to sample user portraits.

02
Full-Stack Systems Design

Brice DataHub

Problem: The BRICE battery circular economy programme at A*STAR generates large volumes of battery research data with no unified platform to manage it. Data was fragmented across ad hoc locations, had no consistent metadata and no access control. Researchers couldn't search, compare, or govern their data effectively.

Process: Designed and built a purpose-built internal data platform on ABP Framework (ASP.NET Core) with a PostgreSQL backend. Direct feedback from biweekly meetings with industry mentors helped shape the end product to better fit requirements.

Outcome: A working platform with a functional Explore page, multi-file upload functionality, scoped row-level Records view, soft deletion, zip-based multi-file download, and role-aware UI.

03
NLP / ML Full-Stack

Cyberbullying Classifier

Problem: Content moderation tools treat harm as binary. Is it harmful or is it safe? This misses the spectrum between offensive language and targeted hate speech, and gives users no signal about why content is flagged.

Process: Fine-tuned DistilBERT on the HateXplain dataset (20,148 posts) for three-class classification. EDA revealed a 51% annotator disagreement rate — an irreducible noise floor that set realistic expectations for the model ceiling. Output strategy: full softmax confidence scores per class rather than hard labels, enabling graduated intervention based on certainty.

Outcome: 69.6% accuracy, macro F1 0.69. Post-training analysis on 919 held-out ambiguous posts found the model systematically less confident on posts humans also disagreed on — a useful property for routing borderline cases to human review. Deployed via FastAPI + React on HuggingFace Spaces.
