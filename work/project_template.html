01 — Overview

What is this project?

Give the context. What is this product or feature? Who uses it, and in what situation? Write this for someone who has never heard of it. 2–4 sentences.

02 — Problem

What needed solving?

State the specific problem clearly. Who was experiencing it? What was the impact? If you have numbers, use them — even rough ones ("users were spending ~20 minutes on a task that should take 2" is more compelling than "it was slow").
